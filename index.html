Privacy Policy

Gym Coach: Offline Workouts (Android package com.rajmalviya.gymcoach)
Last updated: 25 November 2026

In short

We never collect or receive your data. Everything you enter stays on your phone. There is no server for it to go to.

  • You never sign up or log in.
  • The app has nowhere to send your workouts or profile.
  • The app does not track what you do and does not report crashes.
  • No advertising and no advertising trackers.
  • The Android app does not ask for internet access of its own.

What the app stores, and where

Your profile (goal, experience, schedule, equipment, and, only if you choose to enter them, birth year, height and weight), your plan, your workouts and your settings are stored in a database on your phone. The developer cannot see any of it.

Permissions

The app asks for the notification permission, and only when you switch a reminder on. Reminders are set on your phone; no push service is used. The app also uses vibration, restarts its reminders after the phone restarts, and uses Google Play billing for the one-time unlock.

Deleting your data

Because your data exists only on your phone, you delete it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. There is nothing held anywhere else to delete. Backup files you made are yours to keep or delete.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Not medical advice

The app gives general fitness information. It is not medical advice and is not a programme for an injury or condition. Plans come from fixed rules based on general training guidance; no trainer or doctor has reviewed the content.
